- All Delayed shipments have actual delivery dates.
- All In Transit shipments have missing actual delivery dates.

### 4. Data Cleaning Decision

Missing actual_delivery values for In Transit shipments are valid business conditions. They will be preserved rather than deleted or artificially imputed.
